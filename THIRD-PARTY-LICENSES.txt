The legacy UMD build is used because it loads via a classic <script> tag with
no build step (matching this app) and targets older Safari/iOS WebKit. These
two files are NOT precached at install; they are fetched lazily from the app's
own origin on the first report preview and then cached by the service worker.

Notes
